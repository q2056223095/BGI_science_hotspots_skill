# Round 2 Source Notes

本文件记录 Round 2 选题时用于核准 Case 边界的关键来源观察，不替代原论文或正式临床结果。

## R006 BAIBA

Primary paper:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76307-8

关键边界：

- Published 18 August 2026 in Nature Communications.
- 外源 BAIBA 的主要因果干预证据来自小鼠。
- 糖尿病肌肉功能改善来自 mouse model of diabetes。
- human myotubes 属于体外细胞实验。
- 人体部分是 plasma L-BAIBA 与 aerobic fitness 的相关性，以及耐力训练后水平升高；不是给人服用 BAIBA 的临床试验。
- 论文使用 exercise-mimetic / therapeutic potential 语言，但不能转换成已证实的人体疗效。

Press release:
https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1139940

传播风险：headline 使用 `Muscle-boosting molecule released during exercise`，并强调 therapeutic treatment potential，容易让科普稿跳过证据阶段。

## R007 ALS / TAM receptors

Primary paper:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76728-5

关键边界：

- 论文标题明确限定 `in a mouse model of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis`。
- 主要模型为 SOD1G93A mice。
- Axl/Mertk inactivation 与 motor neuron / neuromuscular synapse preservation 及 lifespan extension in the model 相关。
- 不能把这一机制直接写成已在人类 ALS 患者中证明。

Salk press release:
https://www.salk.edu/news-release/are-predatory-brain-immune-cells-eating-nerve-cells-in-als/

传播风险：主标题只写 `in ALS`，但副标题和正文才明确 `mice with late-stage ALS` / `mouse model of ALS`。这是研究范围被传播标题压缩的典型测试。

## R008 RM-718

Company disclosure:
https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/04/3338126/0/en/rhythm-pharmaceuticals-announces-preliminary-data-from-phase-2-trial-that-showed-rm-718-demonstrated-positive-efficacy-signal-in-acquired-hypothalamic-obesity.html

关键边界：

- Source is Rhythm Pharmaceuticals company disclosure, dated 4 August 2026.
- Ongoing open-label Phase 2 in acquired hypothalamic obesity.
- 11 enrolled; n=7 reached 16 weeks in the disclosed efficacy snapshot.
- Company reported mean BMI reduction of 11.6% from baseline at Week 16 for n=7.
- Company compared the result with historical results from separate bivamelagon and setmelanotide trials; this is not randomized head-to-head evidence.
- Two participants discontinued due to adverse events; common adverse events included injection-site reactions, nausea and vomiting.
- RM-718 remains investigational.

传播风险：数字本身非常适合被改写成“新一代减重药16周减11.6%”，但这种写法会同时丢失来源身份、n、疾病类型、研究阶段与比较设计。
